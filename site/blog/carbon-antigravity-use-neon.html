Gemini 4 Carbon has finally arrived on Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo) inside Google Antigravity, sharing the same gemini-weekly quota pool as Gemini 4 Neon and Hydrogen. On paper, Carbon is a capable frontier model—scoring 58.3 at Medium and 60.0 at High on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.

The problem? By the time Carbon reached Google AI Pro, Gemini 4 Neon was already here—and our empirical Cycle 14 & Cycle 15 measurements show that Neon strictly Pareto-dominates Carbon at every reasoning level. Carbon never touches the Pareto frontier.

The Chart: Neon Dominates Carbon Everywhere

Every point below is measured on the same Google AI Pro gemini-weekly pool. Higher is smarter (Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5); further right means more completed tasks per month from your subscription. Notice how the entire Carbon curve sits strictly below and to the left of Neon.

Head-to-Head: Carbon vs. Neon on the Same Weekly Pool

In Cycle 14 (Nov 24, 2026), we ran gemini-4-carbon-medium across our 6-task SWE-bench core suite (4/6 resolved). It consumed 17.69 percentage points of the weekly Gemini pool for $9.90 in API-equivalent tokens—yielding an implied monthly pool value of $242/mo ($206–$279 95% CI). That's right in line with Neon's implied pool value (~$264/mo in Cycle 15).

So why does Carbon lose so badly on monthly tasks? Because Carbon's API pricing ($4.00/$20.00 per 1M tokens) and heavy token footprint push its AA Intelligence Index cost to $6.59–$7.76 per task—roughly 2× to 3.5× more expensive per task than Neon at comparable or higher intelligence.

Even Token-Only Math Confirms It

To make sure this wasn't an artifact of multi-turn harness overhead, we also ran our synthetic 3-prompt quota-weight calibration on Carbon and multiplied by Artificial Analysis's exact per-task token mix. Even in the theoretical token-only upper bound, Carbon Medium tops out at 46 tasks/mo (and Carbon High at 39 tasks/mo)—still far behind Neon High (70 tasks/mo) and Neon Medium (149 tasks/mo).

What Should You Use Instead?

Our recommendation for Google AI Pro Antigravity users hasn't changed—if anything, Carbon's arrival makes it even clearer:

  • Default daily driver: Gemini 4 Neon (Medium) — 54.9 intelligence, ~139 tasks/mo.
  • Fast / high-volume coding: Gemini 4 Neon (Low) — 44.1 intelligence, ~352 tasks/mo.
  • Hard architecture & debugging: Gemini 4 Neon (High) (59.6, ~71/mo) or Neon (xHigh) (61.4, ~41/mo).
  • Gemini 4 Carbon: Skip it unless you have a specific model-preference quirk; on quota and intelligence, Neon already replaced it before it even shipped to Pro.
